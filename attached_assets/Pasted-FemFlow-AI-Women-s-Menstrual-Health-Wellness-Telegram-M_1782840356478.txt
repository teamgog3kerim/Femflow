FemFlow - AI Women's Menstrual Health & Wellness Telegram Mini App

Overview

FemFlow is a modern, AI-powered Telegram Mini App designed to help women monitor, understand, and improve their menstrual health and overall wellbeing. Built entirely within Telegram, it offers a private, intuitive, and multilingual experience (English and Arabic) that combines cycle tracking, wellness insights, personalized AI support, and health education.

Every user who joins FemFlow automatically receives a dedicated AI health companion that learns from their logged information over time to provide personalized guidance, explain menstrual health topics, identify trends, answer questions, and encourage healthy habits. The AI is intended for education and wellness support and clearly advises users to consult healthcare professionals for medical concerns.

Subscription System

FemFlow uses a subscription model.

Plan

Premium Membership

990 ETB per month


Payment Workflow

1. User selects the Premium subscription.


2. The app displays the designated CBE bank account details.


3. The user transfers the subscription fee.


4. The user uploads a payment receipt screenshot and provides basic payment details (such as transfer date and sender name).


5. An administrator reviews the submission.


6. Once approved, the subscription is activated for one month.


7. The user receives confirmation via Telegram.


8. Renewal reminders are sent before expiration.

Vision

FemFlow aims to become a trusted digital women's wellness companion by combining menstrual tracking, AI-powered guidance, wellness education, and personalized insights within the familiar Telegram ecosystem. The goal is to empower women to better understand their bodies, build healthy routines, and make informed decisions about their reproductive health through a simple, intelligent, and accessible platform.
